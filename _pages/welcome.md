# Welcome Guide

You can find information about the group on our website: [https://quantique.labri.fr/](https://quantique.labri.fr/).

### Organisation of the ICQ Group

- **Seminars.** We hold a research seminar on Tuesday mornings at 10:45 (schedule available on the [seminar page]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/seminar/)). You are expected to attend, as it is a central part of your training in quantum computing. You are welcome to suggest topics you would like to see covered or people to invite. Other research groups at LaBRI also hold regular seminars; you can find information about them on the [LaBRI website](https://www.labri.fr/en/research/five-departments).

- **Mailing lists.** You can subscribe to the seminar mailing list [via this link](https://diff.u-bordeaux.fr/sympa/info/labri.gt-info-quantique). You are automatically subscribed to the group mailing lists. The whole group can be reached at [labri.icq@diff.u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:labri.icq@diff.u-bordeaux.fr).

- **Discussion channels.** We have a WhatsApp group and a Slack channel. Ask a permanent member of the group to add you.

- **Shared calendars.** You can add our [seminar calendar](https://webmel.u-bordeaux.fr/home/bf-labri.ca@u-bordeaux.fr/gt.info-quantique.ics) and [visitor calendar](https://webmel.u-bordeaux.fr/home/yassine.hamoudi@u-bordeaux.fr/Visiteurs%20ICQ.ics) to Zimbra, Google Calendar, etc.

- **Git repositories.** The group maintains a private Git repository containing internal documents. Access is restricted to permanent members. The "Maison du Quantique" project has its own Git repository and shared calendar. Ask Yassine Hamoudi for access.

### Resources from LaBRI and the University

- **About LaBRI.** LaBRI is akin to the computer science department of the Université de Bordeaux. It hosts the ICQ group, and this is where (almost) everyone has their office. Some LaBRI members may also be based at [INRIA Bordeaux](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-universite-bordeaux) (a research institute in computer science and applied mathematics) or [Bordeaux INP](https://www.bordeaux-inp.fr/fr) (an engineering school).

- **LaBRI intranet and support.** The [LaBRI intranet](https://www.labri.fr/en/intranet) provides useful resources, including information about research trips and administrative procedures. IT support information is available on the [support website](https://sos.labri.fr/cms/index.php), and requests can be submitted through the [support portal](https://sos-glpi.labri.fr/) (accessible only from within LaBRI; otherwise, email [sos@labri.fr](mailto:sos@labri.fr)). If you have an administrative enquiry and do not know whom to contact, email [accueil@labri.fr](mailto:accueil@labri.fr).

- **University intranet.** The [university intranet](https://intranet.u-bordeaux.fr/) gives you access to your email account and employment information, among other resources (e.g. to request reimbursement of commuting costs).

- **Research trips ("missions").** All research trips must be reported to the LaBRI administration 15 days to one month in advance. The procedure for registering a trip and claiming reimbursement is described in the “Missions” section of the [LaBRI intranet](https://www.labri.fr/intranet) (unofficial [English translation]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/files/mission.pdf)). The procedure depends on whether your trip is funded by the Université de Bordeaux or CNRS, or no reimbursement is requested (ask your supervisor which applies).

- **Holidays.** You must follow the administrative procedures for declaring your holidays. PhD students employed by the university must fill out a paper form available at LaBRI's accueil. Other university employees must use the [Hamac website](https://intranet.u-bordeaux.fr/personnels/espace-RH/organisation-du-travail/horaires-temps-partiel-cet/application-hamac). CNRS employees must use the [Agate website](https://agate-tempo.cnrs.fr/).

- **International researchers.** The university's [Welcome Centre for International Researchers (BACI)](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/en/international/come-to-bordeaux/international-lecturers-researchers-staff/welcome-to-bordeaux) offers a wide range of services for international researchers, including assistance with housing, immigration, and social security. Its [Welcome Guide]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/files/welcome-guide.pdf) is particularly detailed.

### Connecting with People

- **Language and French classes.** We try to communicate in English within the group (and, to some extent, within the lab), but some knowledge of French will be very helpful outside it. If you are a PhD student, the university offers French classes that can count towards your mandatory doctoral training. You can contact [formationdoctorat@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:formationdoctorat@u-bordeaux.fr) for more information (see also the [doctoral training page](https://doctorat.u-bordeaux.fr/en/during-phd/training-during-phd)). Additional options, also open to postdocs and others, are available through [BACI](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/en/international/come-to-bordeaux/international-lecturers-researchers-staff/welcome-to-bordeaux) and its [guide to learning French]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/files/learning-french-bordeaux.pdf).

- **PhD and postdoc association.** There is an association of PhD students and postdocs at LaBRI that organises both social and scientific events. More information is available on [its website](https://afodib.labri.fr/) (an English version is available by clicking "FR" in the top right corner).

- **Mentoring.** LaBRI offers a mentoring programme where you can discuss settling into the lab, career goals, and any difficulties, and receive guidance. Contact [Marthe Bonamy](mailto:marthe.bonamy@u-bordeaux.fr) or [Thomas Degueule](mailto:thomas.degueule@labri.fr).

### Meals

- **Lunch options.** You can have lunch at the university restaurants at a reduced cost. The two most common options are the [Haut-Carré restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4F35neNY9D2jRMZ79) (requires activating an Aquipass card and an Izly account; [see the instructions](https://intranet.u-bordeaux.fr/personnels/vie-universite/vie-sur-les-campus/carte-aquipass)) and the [CNRS restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Es8K9p9H6uhD3hxe7) (requires a CNRS card; contact [suas.bordeaux@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:suas.bordeaux@u-bordeaux.fr)). An Izly account also gives you access to all other [CROUS restaurants](https://www.crous-bordeaux.fr/se-restaurer/ou-manger/), with costs varying according to your status. Some people bring their own lunch and eat together at LaBRI, in the coffee rooms on the 2nd and 3rd floors or the atrium on the 2nd floor.

- **Haut-Carré access for interns.** There is a special procedure for accessing the Haut-Carré restaurant if you are doing an internship at LaBRI *and* your host institution is the Université de Bordeaux. You need to activate a new Izly account, *separate* from any Izly student account you may already have. To do so, email [action.sociale.personnels@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:action.sociale.personnels@u-bordeaux.fr) with the following:

  - The request: "Je suis stagiaire UB et je souhaite faire une demande de subventionnement des repas pris au restaurant du Haut-Carré".
  - A copy of your internship agreement.
  - A new email address that has not been used for another Izly account.

  Once your account is activated, you can collect your card and add money to it at the restaurant's cash desk.

### Housing

- **Housing resources.** The university provides a [housing guide](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/campus/vie-quotidiennne/se-loger) (English option at the top right), though some of the advice applies only to students. [Studapart](https://www.studapart.com/en) has been recommended by several people. [EURAXESS](https://www.euraxess.fr/france/information-assistance/accommodation) also provides useful information, especially for international researchers, and offers some priority housing options. Other platforms that aggregate rental listings from agencies include [OQORO](https://www.oqoro.com/), [Bien’ici](https://www.bienici.com/) and [Jinka](https://www.jinka.fr/).

- **Short-term stays.** For a short-term stay, it may be much simpler (though also more expensive) to look into private student residences ("résidences étudiantes"), which often also accept non-students. Examples include [Studea](https://www.nexity-studea.com/), [YouFirst](https://campus.youfirst.co/fr), [All Suites](https://www.allsuites.com/gamme/all-suites-study), [Study'o](https://www.studyoresidences.fr/), [Ecla](https://www.ecla.com/fr/offre-sejour/bordeaux/), [Yugo](https://yugo.com/fr-fr/global/france/bordeaux), and [Uxco](https://www.uxco.com/fr/logement-etudiant/bordeaux/).

### Medical Support

- **University medical centre.** Appointments with the [university medical centre](https://www.sante-etudiants-bdx.fr/) can be made [here](https://www.doctolib.fr/centre-de-sante/pessac/espace-sante-etudiants-pessac). If no appointment with a doctor is available soon, you can see a nurse (*infirmier*), who can expedite the process if necessary. Doctors known to speak English well include Basile Laridan, Guillaume Conort, Cécile Hoo-Paris, Gaëlle Theux, and Marine Vergnaud. The centre also has translation devices. It is primarily intended for university students (undergraduate, master's, and PhD).

- **Other medical services.** For medical appointments outside the university, you can search on the [Doctolib website](https://www.doctolib.fr/), which lets you filter by the languages spoken by practitioners. This service is not affiliated with the university. Typically, insurance coverage depends on which *secteur* the specialist belongs to (the best option is usually *secteur 1*). Learn more about the [social security system in France](https://www.euraxess.fr/france/information-assistance/social-security). For moderate emergencies, you can get a same-day appointment by calling [SOS Médecins](https://www.sosmedecins-bordeaux.com/english). For medical emergencies, dial 15.

- **Mental health support.** If you are facing mental health challenges, you can reach out to the first-aid team at LaBRI. You can find helpful information on the [PhD and postdoc association's website](https://afodib.labri.fr/problem) and the [LaBRI mental health first-aid page](https://prevention.labri.fr/pssm.html) (accessible with your LaBRI account).
