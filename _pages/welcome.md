### Resources from LaBRI and the University

- The LaBRI is akin to the computer science department of the Université de Bordeaux. It hosts the ICQ group, and this is where everyone has their office. Some people at LaBRI may also be located at [INRIA Bordeaux](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-universite-bordeaux) (research institute in computer science and applied mathematics) or [Bordeaux INP](https://www.bordeaux-inp.fr/fr) (engineering school).

- The [LaBRI intranet](https://www.labri.fr/en/intranet) provides various useful resources (e.g. information about research trips, administrative procedures, etc.). IT support is available at [this link](https://sos.labri.fr/cms/index.php), and requests can be submitted at [this link](https://sos-glpi.labri.fr/) (accessible only from within LaBRI; write to [sos@labri.fr](mailto:sos@labri.fr) otherwise). If you have an administrative inquiry and do not know whom to contact, write to [accueil@labri.fr](mailto:accueil@labri.fr).

- The [university intranet](https://intranet.u-bordeaux.fr/) gives you access, among other things, to your email account and employment information (e.g., to request reimbursement of commuting costs). 

- The university's [Welcome Centre for International Researchers (BACI)](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/en/international/come-to-bordeaux/international-lecturers-researchers-staff/welcome-to-bordeaux) offers a wide range of services for international researchers (assistance with housing, immigration, social security, etc.). Their [Welcome Guide]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/files/welcome-guide.pdf) is particularly detailed.

### Connecting with People

- We try to keep our communications in English within the group (and, to some extent, within the lab), but some French knowledge will be very helpful outside of it. If you are a PhD student, the university offers french-learning classes that can be counted towards your mandatory doctoral training courses. You can reach to [formationdoctorat@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:formationdoctorat@u-bordeaux.fr) for more information (see also [this link](https://doctorat.u-bordeaux.fr/en/during-phd/training-during-phd)). Additional options (accessible to postdocs and others) are available through the [BACI](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/en/international/come-to-bordeaux/international-lecturers-researchers-staff/welcome-to-bordeaux) and [the guide it has published]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/files/learning-french-bordeaux.pdf).

- There is an association of PhD students and postdocs at LaBRI that organizes both social and scientific events. More information is available on [their website](https://afodib.labri.fr/) (English version available by clicking "FR" in the top right corner).

- A mentoring program is available at LaBRI (contact [Marthe Bonamy](mailto:marthe.bonamy@u-bordeaux.fr) or [Thomas Degueule](mailto:thomas.degueule@labri.fr)) to discuss your integration into the lab, career goals, and possible struggles, and to receive guidance.

### Housing and Meals

- You can have lunch at the university restaurants at a reduced cost. The two most common options are the [Haut-Carré restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4F35neNY9D2jRMZ79) (requires activating an Aquipass card and an Izly account; [see how to do](https://intranet.u-bordeaux.fr/personnels/vie-universite/vie-sur-les-campus/carte-aquipass)) and the [CNRS restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Es8K9p9H6uhD3hxe7) (requires a CNRS card; reach to [suas.bordeaux@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:suas.bordeaux@u-bordeaux.fr)). The Izly account grants you access to all other [CROUS restaurants](https://www.crous-bordeaux.fr/se-restaurer/ou-manger/) (costs may vary depending on your status). Some people also bring their lunchbox and gather at LaBRI (coffee rooms on the 2nd and 3rd floors, atrium on the 2nd floor).

- There is a special procedure for accessing the Haut-Carré restaurant if you are doing an internship at LaBRI *and* your hosting institute is Université de Bordeaux. You need to activate a new Izly account, *separate* from the student account you may already have. To do so, write to [action.sociale.personnels@u-bordeaux.fr](mailto:action.sociale.personnels@u-bordeaux.fr) with the following information: say "Je suis stagiaire UB et je souhaite faire une demande de subventionnement des repas pris au restaurant du Haut-Carré", attach a copy of your internship agreement, and provide a new email address that hasn't been used for another Izly account. Once your account is activated, you can get your card and put money on it by going to the cash desk of the restaurant.

- The university provides a [housing guide](https://www.u-bordeaux.fr/campus/vie-quotidiennne/se-loger) (English option at the top right), though some of the advice applies only to students. [Studapart](https://www.studapart.com/en) has been recommended by several people. [EURAXESS](https://www.euraxess.fr/france/information-assistance/accommodation) also provides valuable information, especially for international researchers, and offers some priority housing options. Other platforms that aggregate rental offers from agencies include [Bien’ici](https://www.bienici.com/) and [Jinka](https://www.jinka.fr/). For a one-year stay, it may be much simpler (though also more expensive) to look into private "résidences étudiantes" (which often also accept non-students), such as [Studea](https://www.nexity-studea.com/) or [YouFirst](https://campus.youfirst.co/fr).

### Medical Support

- Appointments with the [university medical center](https://www.sante-etudiants-bdx.fr/) can be made [here](https://www.doctolib.fr/centre-de-sante/pessac/espace-sante-etudiants-pessac). If no early appointment with a doctor is available, it is possible to be seen by an infirmier (nurse), who can expedite the process if deemed necessary. Doctors known to speak English well include, among others, Basile Laridan, Guillaume Conort, Cécile Hoo-Paris, Gaëlle Theux, and Marine Vergnaud. The medical center is also equipped with translation devices. The medical center is primarily intended for university students (undergraduate, master's, and PhD).

- For medical appointments outside the university, you can search on the [Doctolib website](https://www.doctolib.fr/) (there is an option to select the languages spoken by the practitioners) - this service is not affiliated with the university. Typically, insurance coverage depends on which secteur the specialist belongs to (the best option is usually secteur 1). Learn more on the [social security system in France](https://www.euraxess.fr/france/information-assistance/social-security). For moderate emergencies, you can get a same-day appointment by calling [SOS Médecins](https://www.sosmedecins-bordeaux.com/english). For medical emergencies, dial 15.

- If you're facing mental health challenges, you can reach out to the first-aid team at LaBRI. You can find helpful information [here](https://afodib.labri.fr/problem) and [here](https://prevention.labri.fr/pssm.html) (accessible with your LaBRI account).
