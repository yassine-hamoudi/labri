The *Journées Informatique Quantique* (JIQ) is the annual meeting of the CNRS research network in quantum information ([GT IQ](https://members.loria.fr/SPerdrix/gt-iq/)). The goal is to foster exchanges on the main topics of the community (quantum algorithms and complexity, quantum cryptography, quantum information, models of quantum computation and architectures, quantum error correction, quantum simulation, ...).

The meeting will feature invited and contributed talks. Young researchers, especially PhD students and postdocs, are strongly encouraged to present their recent results or ongoing work. Participants who do not submit a talk are also very welcome to attend.

<p style="text-align:center; font-size:30px; font-weight:bold;">Venue</p>

The meeting will take place at [LaBRI](https://www.labri.fr/), located on the campus of the Université de Bordeaux. Location on [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pamdoDBJQtyBhbR16).

Lunches will take place at the university restaurant Haut-Carré (500m from the meeting venue). Location on [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CechEP8ot8dDba9K7).

Registration is <b>free of charge</b> but is required to participate in the event. It covers the lunches on the 15th and 16th. Transportation and accommodation expenses remain the responsibility of the participants.

We have limited funding available to provide financial support for attendees who may need it. Applicants should send an email to <a style="color:#D50032; text-decoration: none;" href="mailto:yassine.hamoudi@labri.fr">yassine.hamoudi@labri.fr</a> before Thursday, 4 December, including a description of the expenses that would need to be covered (transportation, lodging, etc.).
